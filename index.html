FAQ

Straight answers

When does it ship?

First beta goes to the founding list in about 8 weeks. No pre-orders, no upfront charges — we email you the moment it's ready to try.

Is it just loudness?

Yes, deliberately. No noise reduction, no music bed, no AI "enhancement". One job, done right, at broadcast accuracy. If you need full mastering, Auphonic and iZotope RX are excellent — and stay excellent at 10× the price.

Does it work with video files?

Yes. MP4, MOV and friends — the audio track is normalized and remuxed back without re-encoding your video. Zero quality loss on the picture.

Apple Silicon or Intel?

Apple Silicon first; Intel Macs on macOS 13+ supported. Windows: not at launch — we'd rather perfect one platform.
